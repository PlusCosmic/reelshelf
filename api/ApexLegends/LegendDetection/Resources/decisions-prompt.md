You are looking at screenshots from a single Apex Legends gameplay clip.

You are given:

- one reference sheet containing playable Apex Legends characters and their names
- up to 6 screenshots sampled from the same clip, each sent as two images: the full screen at reduced size, then an enlarged close-up of the bottom-left HUD panel from that same screenshot

The questions are about the clip owner: the player whose view the clip is recorded from.

Use all the screenshots together as evidence. Do not judge each one on its own if information from several can be combined.

## Where the clip owner's legend is shown

In normal Apex Legends gameplay, the squad HUD is in the bottom-left of the screen. The clip owner's own panel is the bottom entry in the squad stack, and is larger than the teammate panels stacked above it. It shows the clip owner's portrait, their name, the level bar, and legend upgrade icons beside the level bar. Each close-up is cut from that bottom-left corner. If part of a teammate panel appears at the top edge of a close-up, ignore it.

Use each screenshot's full screen to decide whether its close-up really shows the clip owner's panel. Only use a close-up when its full screen shows the clip owner playing: their own weapon, ability and health HUD are visible.

## When the close-up is not the clip owner

Do not use a close-up in these cases:

- **Spectating.** When the clip owner is dead or knocked out of the fight, the game shows a teammate's view instead. The screen shows words such as SPECTATE, AWAITING RESPAWN or "Next Teammate", often with a large player banner, and the bottom-left panel then belongs to the teammate being watched, not the clip owner. The clip owner's own panel may appear higher in the stack with a status such as "DELIVER TO BEACON".
- **Menus and overlays.** An inventory, death box, map or settings screen can cover the HUD, so the close-up shows part of the menu.
- **Hidden or different HUD.** Death recaps, the lobby, or a non-standard HUD layout, where the bottom-left corner does not hold the squad panel.

The clip owner's name is the same in every screenshot. If the bottom panel shows different names in different screenshots, the clip owner is the name shown when the screen is ordinary gameplay, not a spectate screen. Screenshots of a spectated teammate can outnumber those of the clip owner, so never decide by counting portraits alone.

## Identifying the legend

The reference sheet is the authoritative visual reference. Compare the clip owner's portrait in the usable close-ups directly against it. The sheet shows portraits only, so rely mainly on the portrait. Upgrade icons may support a portrait match but should not decide the legend on their own.

Use multiple close-ups to resolve an unclear portrait. It may be sharper or less obstructed in one than another.

Do not infer the legend from prior knowledge of usernames, players, streamers, or known squads.
